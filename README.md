<h1>TicTacToe </h1>
<h2><bl>Features</bl><h2>
 <P>• 3x3 Grid: Nine clickable squares for placing marks.
<br>
  • Turn System: Alternates automatically between Player X and Player O.
<br>
  • Win & Tie Checks: Detects three-in-a-row victories or a full-board draw.
<br>
  • Game Modes: Supports two local players or playing against a computer AI.
<br>
  • Scoreboard & Reset: Tracks wins and ties, with a button to clear the board.
</P>
